# Kothon privacy policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Kothon is an offline Bengali dictation app made by Omnixima, for macOS, Windows, Linux, iPhone and Android. This policy covers all of them.

## What Kothon collects

Nothing. Kothon has no account, no analytics and no advertising.

## Your voice and text

Speech is recorded and turned into text on your device. The audio and the text are never sent to us or to anyone else. Your dictation history is stored only inside the app on your device, and you can delete it at any time. Deleting the app deletes it too.

## The iPhone keyboard

The Kothon keyboard types without Full Access. If you turn on Full Access, it is used only to pass dictated text between the keyboard and the Kothon app on the same device. The keyboard does not record what you type and makes no network connections.

## Downloads

When you choose to download a speech model, Kothon downloads it from Hugging Face (huggingface.co). This is an ordinary file download, and Kothon sends none of your data with it. Hugging Face may log the request like any web server; see the [Hugging Face privacy policy](https://huggingface.co/privacy). The desktop app also checks GitHub for updates, which sends no personal data.

## Children

Kothon collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. The full history of this page is in this repository.
