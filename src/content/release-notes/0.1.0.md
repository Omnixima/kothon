# কথন 0.1.0

The first release of Kothon: offline Bengali dictation for macOS.

- **Bengali models:** Bangla FastConformer (fast) and BengaliAI Whisper Medium (accurate), running fully on your Mac.
- **Punctuation:** দাঁড়ি, commas and question marks, added on device.
- **Numbers:** spoken numbers written as digits, Bengali or Latin.
- **Learns from your fixes** in History.
- **Banglish and automatic English switching.**
- **History controls:** turn it off, or clear it.
